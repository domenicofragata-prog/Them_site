Wish Room

Your wishes shape your world.

A room, and a machine that listens. Write a wish, a dream, a poem. The machine turns your words into an image, then rebuilds that image out of your own letters, prints it on a sheet and lets it fall into the room.

The sheets gather on the floor, one over the other. Enter the room and find yours: you will know it by your words.

You can write from here, wherever you are. Your wish will be waiting in the room on Saturday night.
